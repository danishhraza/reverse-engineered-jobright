Autofill Jobs — profiles

Everything is stored locally in this browser (chrome.storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Profiles

About you (for the AI)

Facts the AI should take as true when it answers questions: one per line, like I have 1-2 years of professional experience or I don't have a security clearance. Sent with every AI question, along with your salary range and the rest of your profile summary. "All profiles" applies to every resume; a profile's own lines add to them.

Settings

Job site login

Used on sign-up and sign-in pages (Workday, Taleo, iCIMS…) when you press Fill. Saved only in this browser's local storage, not encrypted and never synced, so use a password just for job sites.

AI answers (OpenRouter)

Questions the rules can't answer ("Do you have a relative working at…?") go to a model on OpenRouter with a short summary of your profile (work, education, work authorization, location, skills; no contact details or EEO answers) and the standing answers below. Answers it isn't sure of stay flagged for you. Reused answers are cached.

Learned answers

Questions you answered by hand on flagged fields. These are reused when the exact same question shows up again.

Edit JSON

base is shared by every profile; each profile's overrides replaces just what differs (arrays replace, objects merge).
