Profiles and settings

Everything is stored in this browser only. Nothing leaves it except the questions you let the AI answer.

Profiles

About you, for the AI

Things the AI should take as true when it answers for you, one per line. They go with every AI question, along with your salary range and a summary of your profile. Lines under "All profiles" apply to every resume; a profile's own lines are added on top.

Filling

Job site login

Used on sign-up and sign-in pages (Workday, Taleo, iCIMS and others) when you press Fill. It's kept only in this browser, unencrypted and never synced, so use a password you only use for job sites.

AI answers

Questions the rules can't answer ("Do you have a relative working at…?") go to a model on OpenRouter, with a short summary of your profile: work, education, work authorization, location and skills. Contact details and EEO answers are never sent. Answers it's unsure of are marked for you to review, and repeat questions reuse earlier answers.

Learned answers

Answers you typed into flagged fields. They're reused when the exact same question comes up again.

Edit JSON

base is shared by every profile. Each profile's overrides replaces only what differs: lists are replaced, objects are merged.
